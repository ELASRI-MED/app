01 — About

Where AI meets
Earth Observation

I am a doctoral researcher developing hybrid deep learning architectures to classify agricultural crops from multi-temporal Sentinel-1 SAR satellite imagery — bridging artificial intelligence with Earth observation to support precision agriculture across Morocco and the wider African continent.

Alongside my research, I hold an administrative role at the École Supérieure de Technologie in Essaouira, and I am involved in applied innovation through the SolHydra precision agriculture startup.

I am passionate about making satellite data more accessible and actionable for farmers, policymakers, and agri-businesses.

CNN2D-Mamba Hybrid Architecture for Crop Classification
A novel deep learning architecture combining 2D convolutional networks with the Mamba state-space model for multi-temporal Sentinel-1 SAR crop classification over Morocco's Tadla and Gharb plains. Target crops include wheat, sugar beet, and maize. Forms the core of my doctoral thesis.
